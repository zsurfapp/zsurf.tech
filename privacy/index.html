PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy,
fully explained.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

At zsurf.tech, we place the highest importance on protecting the privacy and personal information of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains, in clear and comprehensive terms, what kinds of information may be collected when you use our website, how that information is used, stored, and shared, and what rights you have as a user of zsurf.tech.

1. Information we collect

When you visit and use zsurf.tech, certain technical information may be collected automatically to ensure the proper operation, security, and improvement of the website. This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Referring page and exit page
  • Pages visited, links clicked, and approximate time spent on each page
  • Date and time of each visit
  • General geographic location derived from the IP address

We use this information to monitor website performance, analyze traffic and user behavior patterns, improve the quality of our published content, and enhance the overall user experience. None of this information is used to personally identify individual visitors unless required by law.

2. Cookies

zsurf.tech uses cookies for analytics and advertising purposes. Specifically, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website, and Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help us recognize your browser on subsequent visits and remember certain preferences. You may choose to accept or decline non-essential cookies through our cookie consent banner. You may also configure your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time.

Third-party advertising cookies used by Google AdSense may be used to personalize the ads shown to you based on your browsing behavior. Google’s use of advertising cookies is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

3. Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to our newsletter, we collect and store your email address with our email service provider in order to deliver newsletter content to you. Each newsletter email contains an unsubscribe link that allows you to opt out at any time. Once you unsubscribe, your email address is removed from our active mailing list.

4. Contact form

When you submit a message through our contact form, we collect the information you provide (such as your name, email address, subject, and message content) for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not use this information for marketing purposes or share it with third parties.

5. Third-party services

To operate and improve zsurf.tech, we rely on the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google Analytics , for aggregated, anonymized traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense , for displaying relevant advertisements
  • Hosting provider , for serving website content securely

These third-party services may collect information governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies to understand how your data is handled.

6. How we protect your information

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict the processing of, or request portability of your personal data. If you are located in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Data retention

We retain analytics data for a period of 26 months, in line with common industry practice. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained only for as long as necessary to address your inquiry.

9. Children’s privacy

zsurf.tech is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we may delete the information promptly.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

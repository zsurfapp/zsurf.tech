Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

zsurf.tech ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Automatically collected: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this for analytics and to improve our content.

Cookies: We use cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense). You can decline non-essential cookies using our cookie banner.

Newsletter: If you subscribe, we store your email address with our email provider to deliver the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

How we use information

  • To deliver and improve our content
  • To analyze traffic and trends
  • To personalize ads shown via Google AdSense
  • To respond to your inquiries

Third-party services

We use Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services may collect information governed by their own privacy policies. Google's advertising standards require us to disclose that we use cookies for ads personalization.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. California residents have similar rights under the CCPA. Contact us at privacy@zsurf.tech to exercise these rights.

Data retention

We retain analytics data for 26 months. Newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.

Children's privacy

zsurf.tech is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@zsurf.tech.

How to Disable Autocorrect on Android: 3 Proven Methods

Autocorrect on Android is more aggressive than ever, and the new AI powered prediction in Gboard makes mistakes that the older system would not have. Here is how to tame it without losing the productivity benefits.

Why autocorrect fails

The new AI prediction runs on device and learns from your typing. It tries to predict the next word based on context, but the predictions are sometimes hilariously wrong. The fix is to teach it better or replace it.

Disable autocorrect in Gboard

Open Gboard settings, go to Text correction, and toggle off Auto correction. You can also disable Suggestion, which prevents the predictive text bar from showing. The keyboard now only fixes obvious typos.

Disable autocorrect entirely on Samsung

Samsung Keyboard has its own settings. Open the keyboard, tap the gear icon, and find Smart typing. Toggle off Auto replace, Auto punctuate, and Suggest next word. The keyboard becomes much less intrusive.

Add words to the dictionary

The fastest way to make autocorrect smarter is to teach it. Type the word correctly three times and it gets added to your personal dictionary. You can also add words manually via Settings, Languages, Personal dictionary.

Voice typing is now excellent on Pixel and Samsung phones. The on device speech recognition is faster than cloud based and more private. For long messages, voice typing with light editing is faster than keyboard typing.

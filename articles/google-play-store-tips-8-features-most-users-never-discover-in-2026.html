Google Play Store Tips: 8 Features Most Users Never Discover

The Google Play Store is the gateway to most Android apps. Most users only use search, but there are many features that make finding, evaluating, and installing apps much better. Here are the tips that will save you time and money.

Use the filters properly

Tap the filter icon next to the search bar. You can filter by rating, downloads, last updated, in app purchases, ads, and content rating. Filtering by Last updated within 6 months helps you avoid abandoned apps.

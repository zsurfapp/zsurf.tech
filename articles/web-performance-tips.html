12 Web Performance Wins That Actually Matter

Skip the framework debates. These 12 measurable optimizations will move your Lighthouse score from 60 to 95+ — and they don't require rewriting your app.

1. Compress everything

Enable Brotli or Gzip on your origin or CDN. Text payloads shrink 60–80%.

2. Defer non-critical CSS

Inline critical CSS, lazy-load the rest. Your first paint drops dramatically.

3. Use modern image formats

AVIF and WebP cut image weight by 30–50% vs JPEG/PNG.

4. Set explicit width/height on images

Prevents layout shift (CLS). Browsers reserve space before the image loads.

5. Preconnect to third-party origins

<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com"> saves 100ms+ on first requests.

6. Code-split your bundles

Route-level code splitting with React.lazy or dynamic imports. Users download only what they need.

7. Use a CDN

Cloudflare, Fastly, BunnyCDN — pick one. Latency drops to your users.

8. HTTP/3 + early hints

103 Early Hints let the browser preload assets before the response body arrives.

9. Cache aggressively

Long-lived cache headers + content hashing = repeat visits are instant.

10. Lazy-load below-the-fold images

loading="lazy" everywhere except hero images.

11. Reduce JavaScript

Audit your bundles. Every 100KB of JS on mobile costs ~1s of TTI.

12. Measure in the real world

Lighthouse is a starting point. Real User Monitoring (RUM) tells you what users actually experience.

Performance isn't a one-time project. It's a habit. Measure, optimize, ship, repeat.

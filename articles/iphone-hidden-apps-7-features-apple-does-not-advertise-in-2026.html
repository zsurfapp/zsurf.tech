iPhone Hidden Apps: 7 Features Apple Does Not Advertise

Apple ships a number of hidden and pre installed apps on every iPhone that most users never open. Some are genuinely useful. Some are placeholders for features not yet enabled in your region. We spent two weeks cataloguing every hidden app and what it actually does.

Apple includes a Compass app, a Measure app, a Translate app, a Magnifier, and several others that do not appear in the App Store but ship with iOS. Each one is a fully featured tool that most users ignore.

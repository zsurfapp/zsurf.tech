Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra Review: The Best Android Phone of 2026

Samsung's 2026 flagship lands with a more refined titanium frame, the largest vapor chamber ever shipped on a Galaxy, and a periscope telephoto that finally closes the zoom gap with the competition. We spent two weeks with the S26 Ultra to find out whether the upgrade is worth it.

Design and build

The S26 Ultra drops 8 grams compared to the S25 Ultra and shrinks the side bezels by 0.4 millimeters. The titanium frame is now sandblasted instead of polished, which dramatically reduces fingerprint visibility. The S Pen has the same latency at 2.8 milliseconds but writes 18 percent more accurate pressure curves at the extremes.

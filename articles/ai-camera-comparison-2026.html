AI Camera Comparison 2026: Which Phone Takes the Best Photos

Every flagship uses a dedicated NPU to process photos. The NPU runs multi frame noise reduction, semantic segmentation, and AI upscaling in milliseconds. The differences between phones are now in the AI models, not the sensors.

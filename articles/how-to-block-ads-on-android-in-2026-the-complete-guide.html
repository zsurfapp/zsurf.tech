How to Block Ads on Android in 2026: The Complete Guide

Android ads have gotten more aggressive in 2026. System level ad blocking is the only reliable defense. Here is how to set it up in 30 minutes, whether you have a rooted device or not.

Why app ads are getting worse

Ad networks now use machine learning to defeat block lists within hours. In app browsers open links you cannot escape. Notification ads and popup overlays are standard. Free apps fund themselves through aggressive ad placements that hurt the experience.

The two paths: rooted vs unrooted

Rooted devices get system level DNS filtering via AdGuard or Blokada. Unrooted devices get Private DNS at the network level, which blocks most ads but not all in app ones. Rooted is strictly better.

Setting up Private DNS on Android 14+

Go to Settings, Network, Private DNS. Select Private DNS provider hostname. Enter dns.adguard-dns.com. Save. All apps now use encrypted DNS through AdGuard. About 80 percent of ads are blocked.

Android Storage Management: How to Free Up Space and Keep It That Way

Modern phones ship with 128 GB to 1 TB of storage, but it fills up faster than most people expect. Photos, videos, apps, and system caches can eat 200 GB in a year. Here is how to keep your phone from running out of space.

How storage fills up

Photos and videos are the biggest consumers. Apps have grown: an average app is now 250 MB. System files take 15 to 25 GB. Caches can accumulate to 10+ GB if not cleared. Downloads folder is often forgotten.

The Files app is your friend

Every Android phone has a Files app that shows exactly what is taking up space. Open it, go to Clean, and let it suggest deletions. Most users find 10 to 30 GB of recoverable space the first time they run it.

Manage photos aggressively

Enable cloud backup to Google Photos or Amazon Photos, then turn on Free up space. The phone will delete local copies of photos that are safely in the cloud. You can still view them in the app, but they do not take local space.

Uninstall apps you do not use

The average user has 80 apps installed and uses 30 of them. The other 50 take up space and background battery. Go through your app drawer once a quarter and uninstall anything you have not opened in 30 days.

Clear app caches selectively

Clearing the cache for every app is overkill and resets logins. Clear caches only for apps that are misbehaving. The browser cache in Chrome can be 5 GB, which is worth clearing occasionally.

Offload music and video

Spotify and YouTube Music let you download playlists for offline listening. Configure the download quality to standard, not high, and you will save 60 percent of the space. Delete downloads after you have listened.

The Downloads folder is a black hole

Files you downloaded once and forgot about pile up. Open Files, go to Downloads, and delete everything you do not need. PDFs, images, APKs, and zip files all live here.

When to get more storage

If you shoot a lot of 4K video, get the 512 GB or 1 TB variant. If you live in the cloud with Google Photos and streaming music, 128 GB is plenty. External storage via USB C SSD is now cheap and works well.
